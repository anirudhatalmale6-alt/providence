Your property. Professionally managed.

Providence works with a small number of selected owners to operate their property as professionally managed serviced accommodation.

We are not a letting agent and we are not a listing site. We take responsibility for the presentation of the property, for the people who stay in it and for its condition when they leave — and we report on all three.

Arrangements differ by property. Some owners prefer a fixed monthly figure paid whether or not the apartment is occupied; others prefer to retain management and have us provide the hosting. We are glad to discuss either.

How your property is looked after

Property care

Professional cleaning

Every changeover cleaned to a written standard, with linen laundered off site.

Guest screening

Guests are verified before arrival, with house rules agreed in writing.

Regular inspections

The property is inspected on a schedule, not only when something goes wrong.

Responsive maintenance

Small repairs handled promptly; anything larger referred to you with photographs.

Presentation standards

Interiors kept to the standard the property was let in, and refreshed as needed.

Professional communication

One point of contact, and a straight answer when you ask how it is going.

Compliance

Certification, insurance and any permissions confirmed in writing before we begin.

Reporting

A regular written update on occupancy, condition and anything requiring your decision.

Partner with us

Let’s talk about your property

Tell us where the property is and how it is currently let. If it is not right for Providence we will say so plainly rather than waste your time.

Before any arrangement begins

We confirm in writing that the necessary permissions are in place — the lease, any mortgage conditions and the appropriate insurance. It protects you as much as it protects us.
